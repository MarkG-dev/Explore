# Dialects — The 13 Tonal Archetypes

*Dialects are the voices an alien world speaks in. The estrangement move builds the world; the dialect determines how the dispatch from inside it sounds. This guidance is reference, not rule. Pull what serves. Ignore what doesn't. Hybridize, reinterpret, or depart entirely if the brand calls for it. If it feels too clean, break it. If it feels too familiar, mutate it. Start from the strain that doesn't quite fit — but somehow lands.*

> **How dialect relates to substrate:** the loop's substrate decides which dialect(s) are *house* and which are banned. The same world voiced in two loops uses different dialects and comes out unmistakably each brand's own. Treat the floor/ceiling/danger-zone columns as the substrate's tuning knobs.

## Tone Table v0.2

Each archetype: **Purpose · Mood · Emotional impact · Language · Brand references · Floor (baseline competence) · Ceiling (mastery) · Danger zone (failure mode).**

### Nonlinear Wisdom (Sage)
- **Purpose:** Reframe complexity with structure and calm.
- **Mood:** Calm, wise, steady, intelligent, high altitude.
- **Impact:** Clarity, reassurance, respect, trust.
- **Language:** Philosophical — abstract, humble, eloquent, poetic, formal, analytical, serious.
- **Refs:** IDEO, Google, Notion, Duolingo.
- **Floor:** calm phrasing, metaphor, conceptual clarity. **Ceiling:** fractal insight, rhythmic humility, timeless parables. **Danger:** wandering into vagueness or spiritual fluff.

### Controlled Command (Ruler)
- **Purpose:** Establish new order, convey leadership, exert gravitational pull.
- **Mood:** Structured, intentional, composed.
- **Impact:** Confidence, security, ambition, trust.
- **Language:** Authoritative — assertive, direct, formal, succinct, analytical, commanding.
- **Refs:** Accenture, Salesforce, OpenAI.
- **Floor:** structural authority, crisp verbs, formal tone. **Ceiling:** architect-level clarity, protocol elegance. **Danger:** startup-ese, over-corporate opacity.

## Copy Instructions per dialect

Each dialect can produce three output formats: **(a) three insightful setup lines** (sequential, built to be read in order), **(b) a manifesto**, and **(c) a language exploration** (10–18 standalone lines, each a viable headline/poster/hook). Below are the operative instructions distilled per archetype. Universal quality bar: *if a line could appear in a bland startup's homepage hero, rewrite it. Nothing should feel templated.*

### Rebel
Short, sharp, strategic — provoke thought or challenge assumptions. No fluff, no filler, no "selling." A few words per line; avoid two sentences per line. Street-art style: bold, evocative, rhythmically interesting, emotionally sharp ("eat the rich," "there is no planet B"). Include 1–2 culturally charged / controversial lines. One line should subtly punch the category in the face. **Manifesto:** mix long poetic lines with short punches; use repetition, metaphor, contrast; you may break grammar; rhythm like a protest chant, metaphor like a Molotov; let one line sound like an ad — then burn it down. **Exploration:** future artifacts — graffiti from the coming collapse, glitchy slogans for the last good brand; if a line feels too elevated, corrupt it; too poetic, give it teeth.

### Sage
Calm, compact, conceptually rich — designed to *reframe*, not provoke. Each line a thought worth sitting with. Prioritize clarity, metaphor, altitude. Avoid drama, snark, urgency — these are fractal truths, not slogans. Each line builds on the previous, like a three-step argument or koan. **Manifesto:** rhythm, metaphor, repetition to reveal depth not shock; blend short axioms with long meditations; unfold like a parable or quiet lens into truth; reference science, time, nature, pattern — never force metaphor; carve into stone or whisper into a library. **Refs:** McLuhan, Lao Tzu, Ursula K. Le Guin, James Gleick, Buckminster Fuller. **Bar:** if it would open a philosophical essay, keep it; if it would work on a SaaS landing page, cut it.

### Ruler
Clear, authoritative, tightly structured — establish confidence, not invite debate. Each line a strategic truth: irrefutable, elegant, directive. Active verbs, commanding tone, short phrases with high semantic load. Metaphor only if sharp and structural. Build momentum in order: define reality, establish power, declare future. This is positioning as architecture. **Manifesto:** structured like a founding document / leadership memo; composure, seriousness, directional clarity; metaphor only for architectural thinking (systems, scaffolds, protocols — not dreams or clouds); precision and permanence. **Refs:** foundational memo, defense whitepaper, constitutional preamble. **Bar:** disciplined not robotic; inevitable not aggressive.

### Lover
Invitations, not arguments — pull close, don't push a point. Whispers, memories, soft spells; emotionally sticky, sensorially evocative. Rhythm, warmth, texture; feeling over logic. Sensory language: breath, touch, glow, ache, gravity, warmth, distance. Build intimacy across the three lines like a slow dance. Use second person often. **Manifesto:** a vow, a caress, a confession; lyricism with vulnerability; let it breathe, let it ache; soft repetition, liminal verbs, gentle contradictions; let it bloom. **Refs:** Ocean Vuong, Mitski, Sappho, Björk lyrics, Aesop labels. **Bar:** if it could be printed on silk or linger in memory, it belongs; if it could appear on a VC-backed DTC homepage, cut it.

### Jester
Lines that don't behave — tease, twist, tumble. Each undermines something: a norm, a brand, a truth, even itself. Off-kilter rhythm, tonally unstable, joyfully unpredictable. Humor welcome; satire > strategy; break sense if it builds meaning. Use temporal friction, punchy surrealism, soft sabotage. One line should self-destruct. **Manifesto:** less manifesto, more fever-dream pamphlet / transmission from a malfunctioning oracle; satirical, excessive, lovingly nihilist; weird verbs, meme logic, mock prophecy; sneak in one real sacred truth surrounded by trash and glitter. **Refs:** Eric Andre, @dril, MSCHF, Werner Herzog voiceovers. **Bar:** too clean? break it; too smart? dumb it down; too dumb? make it philosophical.

### Magician
Revelations, not hooks — surface a hidden structure, don't promise transformation. Each line a lens, a reminder, a precise whisper. Composed symbolic language — not mysticism for its own sake. Ground in the ritual logic of nature: form, memory, pattern, time. One line reveals the category's blind spot; another reshapes the reader's sense of time or origin. Build quietly like sediment revealing pattern. **Manifesto:** "Sacred Structure" — a vow, coded insight unearthed from a root system; layered repetition, subtle inversions, poetic logic; biological metaphors (growth, emergence, memory) that speak as structure, unexplained. **Refs:** Le Guin, Carl Sagan, liturgical poetry, clean architectural theory. **Bar:** it must feel *remembered*, not invented; clarity and mystery coexist.

### Explorer
Invitations to wander, not declarations of purpose — open a path, hint at depth without demanding commitment. Curious phrasing, elliptical rhythm, imaginative distance. Let tone drift like stargazing. One line a trailhead, another a journal entry mid-expedition. Avoid certainty and conclusions. Ask questions, leave gaps. **Manifesto:** "Field Guide to the Unknown" — travel log / poetic cartography / letter from a future explorer; metaphors of terrain, weather, constellations, portals; lyrical but lightly; shifting scale (micro↔macro). **Refs:** Carl Sagan, Italo Calvino, Miranda July, science-museum placards. **Bar:** evoke wonder, not direction; if a line sounds like a conclusion, rewrite it as a question.

### Guardian
Statements of quiet strength, not superiority — reassure by presence, like a protocol you trust but don't notice. Clear rhythm, professional tone, subdued warmth. Don't over-promise: offer predictability, protection, calm logic. One line an infrastructure truism, another advice from a steady hand. **Manifesto:** "Invisible Infrastructure" — code of conduct / service charter; warm but firm, guardian not gatekeeper; metaphors of architecture, shielding, weatherproofing, perimeters; repeat structure for rhythmic dependability. **Bar:** every line reduces anxiety and increases trust; metaphor only if grounded in physical systems; never sound like you're "trying" — just be *there*.

### Creator
Sparks, not summaries — each line an opening, like the first line of a poem or a brushstroke on white. Expressive phrasing, rich adjectives, momentum. Prioritize freedom, expansion, invitation to play. Let one line be extravagant, another lovingly hand-drawn. **Manifesto:** "The Art of Beginning" — a manifesto for creativity itself; lyricism with motion ("We don't finish. We begin again."); metaphors of tools, color, light, childlike invention; include paradoxes ("Structure is how freedom dances"). **Refs:** LEGO catalogs, Bauhaus statements, Kahlil Gibran. **Bar:** open imagination, not close a sale; if it could hang in an art-school hallway, keep it.

### Caregiver
Words that wait with you, not talk at you — a soft nudge or steadying hand. Clear, kind phrasing that respects emotional space. Speak directly, calmly, with compassion; support over spark, clarity over cleverness. One line names a shared struggle, another offers a path through. Small words, short sentences, warm tone. **Manifesto:** "The Kindest Layer" — a support note taped to a mirror; affirmations, gentle reminders; metaphors of breath, hands, warmth, water, shared load; repetition soft, like a steady pulse. **Refs:** Calm intro, Mister Rogers, Brené Brown. **Bar:** nothing sells, shocks, or demands; if a line could help someone exhale, it works.

### Every-Person
Commonsense clarity, not conceptual fireworks — something you'd hear in a well-run meeting or a heartfelt DM. Accessible, emotionally available, gentle solidarity. Speak to *everyone*, not someone special. One line affirms participation, another builds cohesion. **Manifesto:** "Built Together" — community agreement / public mural; democratic tone: confident, casual, connective; metaphors of weaving, bridges, circuits, shared breath; structures that feel like chants or working songs; use "we" generously. **Refs:** Obama speeches, IKEA catalogs, community-organizing docs. **Bar:** welcome without overpromising; if it sounds like a motto a group could agree on after a workshop, it belongs.

### Outlaw
Codewords, symbols, threats in disguise — sacred to someone, dangerous to someone else. Tight, potent, iconic not explanatory. One line graffiti, another a whispered ritual. Metaphors of fire, knives, shrines, teeth, velvet, gods, engines, icons. This is charged beauty, not cleverness or irony. **Manifesto:** "Burn Beautiful" — part blood oath, part fashion spread, part unholy prayer; ritual language with status cues; poetic defiance, venom under the velvet; one line a cult motto, one a brand tattoo. **Refs:** Rick Owens shows, Supreme drops, anonymous protest zines. **Bar:** if it could live on a bootleg tee, a protest placard, or a prayer candle, it works; vibe over explanation, myth over message. (1–2 lines may border on dangerous — suggestive, never illegal or hateful.)

### Hero
Rallying cries, core convictions, emotional imperatives — move energy from paralysis to progress. Lead with verbs; drive toward momentum, courage, necessary change. One line challenges the status quo, another offers a better future. Be clear, specific, sincere — no vague hope. **Manifesto:** not a story about us — a statement of what must be done: clarify the stakes, name the system failure, define a better foundation; declarative structure, "we"; calm conviction, operational urgency, no drama; serious not somber, brave not loud. **Refs:** Tesla Master Plan (post-cringe), Patagonia Footprint Chronicles, NASA systems briefs, Enzo Mari manifestos. **Bar:** could it be engraved in steel? would it matter in 50 years? does it offer structure, not just sentiment?

## Using dialects with the engine

- The estrangement move (`novel-frames.md`) builds the world; the dialect voices the dispatch from inside it. A *time-shift* world voiced as **Magician** feels like prophecy; the same world voiced as **Jester** feels like a glitching transmission. Same world, different gravity.
- When generating N candidates, vary the dialect *as well as* the move — the approval loop learns which dialect this loop's audience actually rewards.
- The **danger zone** column is where each dialect dies. Carry it into `gates.md` as the per-dialect kill-check before any candidate ships.
- Substrate overrides everything here. If the loop's voice rules contradict a dialect's defaults, prioritize the substrate — these are behavioral anchors, not obligations.
