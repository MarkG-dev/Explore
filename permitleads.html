Talk to us
Questions

Straight answers.

Are these leads really exclusive?

Yes. During the pilot each scored lead goes to one contractor per trade in a territory — the opposite of the shared-lead model. The permit record is public, but the scoring, ranking, and adjacent-trade matching are what you're paying for.

Where does the data come from?

Public building-permit records filed with Berkshire County towns (PermitEyes and municipal portals). When someone pulls a permit, it's a public announcement that a funded, approved project is starting at a specific address.

Is contacting these homeowners allowed?

You contact them the way you already do — the permit is public record. We surface the lead; you make the outreach. We keep the pilot deliberately simple and compliant on this front.

What if the leads don't convert?

That's exactly what the free 30-day pilot is for. If scored permit leads don't beat what you're doing now, you walk away having spent nothing.

Which trades do you cover?

Starting with roofing, solar, and HVAC/remodeling in Berkshire County. If you're an adjacent trade (gutters, insulation, electrical), tell us — you may already be sitting on our opportunity map.
