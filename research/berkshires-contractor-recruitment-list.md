**Compiled:** July 1, 2026
**Trades:** Roofing · Solar · HVAC / General Remodeling
**Target:** ~20–30 genuinely small (<20 employee), local / owner-operator shops physically based in or clearly serving Berkshire County.

> Source: direct web research (business-listing snippets, Yelp/BBB/Buzzfile, MassCEC, GAF/Owens Corning directories, Google Business). Many company sites 403-blocked automated fetches, so some fields come from third-party listings. Treat as a **recruiting starter list, not a vetted vendor list** — re-confirm every phone/email before outreach.

## Methodology & Caveats

- Every row is traceable to a public source. Fields that could not be confirmed are left blank rather than guessed; low-confidence fields are marked **(verify)**.
- Phone numbers came from listings — re-confirm against the company's own site or a call before outreach; directory data goes stale.
- Size signals are inferences from public cues (owner-operator language, "family-owned," years in business, small review counts). No shop publishes exact headcount, so "small / <20 employees" is directional, not confirmed.
- Exclusions: large national chains, franchise roll-ups, and lead-gen aggregators (Power Home Remodeling, SunRun, Trinity Solar, Momentum, EnergySage/Angi/BuildZoom listing pages) were left out. Borderline regional players are flagged in notes.

## Gaps / Next Steps

**Trade balance is uneven — solar is thinnest.**
- **Roofing:** good coverage (5–6 solid small shops).
- **HVAC / remodel:** strongest (9+ shops).
- **Solar:** weakest — only **BPVS** is a confidently small, Berkshire-based installer. PV Squared serves the county but is a larger Greenfield co-op. Most solar hits were regional/national (ReVision, Viridis, Ion Solar Pros, Northeast Solar, All Energy Solar) or aggregators. **Dig manually here.**

**Town coverage gaps** — list skews to Pittsfield/Lenox/Williamstown. Thin/missing based-in-town shops: **Great Barrington, Sheffield, Lee, Adams, Dalton, Stockbridge, Cheshire, Becket, Hinsdale**. South County (Great Barrington/Sheffield) deserves its own pass.

**Where to dig manually:**
1. **MA HIC (Home Improvement Contractor) & CSL registration directory** (mass.gov license lookup) — searchable by town; authoritative for small local registered contractors. Highest-value next step for filling town gaps.
2. **Municipal building-permit records** (Pittsfield, Great Barrington, North Adams building departments) — see which small contractors are *actually pulling permits locally* (directly relevant to a permit-lead pilot).
3. **1Berkshire / Berkshire Chamber of Commerce** member directory — good for South County and solar.
4. **MassCEC approved-installer list** and **EnergySage / NABCEP** directories — additional small Berkshire solar shops beyond BPVS.
5. **Verify all (verify)-flagged fields** and re-confirm every phone before outreach.

**Data-quality reminders for outreach:**
