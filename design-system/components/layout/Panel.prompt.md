Signature chamfered container. Use for cards, framed media, and content blocks. Corners are cut (never rounded).

```jsx
<Panel tone="dark" bordered padding={32}>…</Panel>
<Panel tone="muted">…</Panel>
```

Tones: `light`, `dark`, `muted` (#F7F7F7), `grey` (#C4C4C4), `none`. Pair `bordered` with a hairline. Adjust `chamfer` for the cut-corner size.
