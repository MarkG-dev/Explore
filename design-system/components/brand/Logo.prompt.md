Loop X logo — the official wordmark lockup and the standalone X symbol. Use the wordmark by default; use the symbol only where space is extremely tight (favicons, app icons, avatars).

```jsx
<Logo height={48} tone="var(--lx-off-white)" />
<Logo variant="symbol" height={32} />
```

Paints in `currentColor` — place on black or white and set `color`/`tone`. Never alter, redraw, stretch, or decorate the mark. Keep strong contrast against the background.
