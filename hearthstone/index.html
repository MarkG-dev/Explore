Longitudinal Study · 2013–2026

Every card, every meta.
Thirteen years of Hearthstone ranked.

A complete, downloadable database of every card in Hearthstone Constructed — dated not by its set label but by the first game build it ever appeared in, reconstructed from 320 dated client snapshots. Plus the balance-change history of every card, the mechanics taxonomy, and an era-by-era study of the ranked ladder.

Card data datamined from the Hearthstone client via HearthSim/hsdata. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. Hearthstone is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.
