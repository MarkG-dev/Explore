Action log

The one new rule. A Response is armed face-down on your own turn, paid for with Guard. On your opponent's turn you get exactly one chance to fire it, and you choose which of its two modes at that moment — that choice is the thing Hearthstone's pre-committed Secrets never let you make.

Getting Guard is the real cost. Guard comes only from mana you did not spend: at end of turn, every 2 unspent mana becomes 1 Guard, capped at 3. So holding a Response means deliberately underdeveloping. That lossy 2:1 rate is what stops "always hold Guard" being free value.

Your opponent always sees your Guard total and whether you are armed — but never which card. That visibility is the point: it is what lets them play around you.
